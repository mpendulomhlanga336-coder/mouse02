Welcome to my website about the impact of online banking services on consumers in Johannesburg.

This website presents a summary of my investigation into how the transition from traditional banking to online banking affects financial security, accessibility and different groups of consumers.


Introduction

In 2026, online banking has become an important part of everyday financial activities. Consumers can use banking applications, websites and USSD services to perform transactions without visiting a physical bank branch.

This report investigates the impact of online banking services on consumers, highlighting both the positive and negative effects. Online banking provides benefits such as convenience, accessibility and the ability to perform transactions at any time. However, consumers also face challenges such as cyber-fraud, phishing, unreliable internet access, data costs and a lack of digital literacy.

Focus Question

To what extent does the transition to online banking in 2026 impact the financial security and accessibility of diverse consumer groups within the Johannesburg metropolitan area?


Accessibility

Investigating whether different consumer groups have equal access to online banking services.

Security

Examining online banking risks such as phishing, social engineering and cyber-fraud.

Consumer Impact

Exploring how online banking has changed the financial habits and experiences of consumers.


Why Does This Matter?

Online banking can make financial services more convenient and accessible, but the benefits are not experienced equally by all consumers.

Cyber-fraud, high data costs, limited internet access and differences in digital literacy can create barriers for certain groups.
