Findings & Analysis

This section presents the key findings from the investigation into the impact of online banking services on consumers in Johannesburg. It is found that online banking has changed the way consumers access and manage their finances. Digital banking provides greater convenience and can improve access to financial services, but it also creates new challenges for consumers.

1. Growth of Online Banking

The adoption of online banking services has grown significantly in Johannesburg over the past decade. Many consumers now prefer to use digital platforms for their banking needs due to the convenience and accessibility they offer. Consumers can use smartphoneapplications, websites and USSD services to perform transactions without visiting a physical bank branch.

The research in this investigation indicates that there were approximately 26.5 million active digital banking users in South Africa in early 2026, with a significant portion of these users located throughout the country. This growth is attributed to the increasing availability of internet access, the proliferation of smartphones, and the convenience of online banking services.
this may include:

  • Online banking platforms
  • Mobile banking applications
  • Online banking websites
  • USSD banking services
  • Real-time digital payments

2. Convenience and Accessibility

Online banking services provide consumers with the convenience of managing their finances from anywhere at any time. Consumers can check account balances, transfer funds, pay bills, and access financial information without the need to visit a bank branch. This convenience is particularly beneficial for consumers with busy schedules or those living in remote areas.

Online banking can provide access to financial services 24 hours a day and can reduce the need to wait in queues. It may also provide lower fees for certain services.
this may include:

  • Available 24 hours a day
  • Reduces the need to visit branches
  • Allows faster transactions
  • Can reduce certain banking costs

3. Security Concerns



Security is one of the major concerns associated with online banking. The investigation found that consumers can be targeted by phishing, social engineering and other forms of cyber-fraud.

The research indicates that many cases of digital banking fraud involve consumers being manipulated into providing personal or banking information.

    this may include:
  • Phishing
  • Social engineering
  • SIM-swap scams
  • Fraudulent messages
  • Attempts to obtain OTPs and personal information

4. Digital Divide

The investigation found that not all consumers have equal access to online banking services. Some consumers may face challenges due to limited internet access, lack of digital literacy, or financial constraints. This digital divide can create disparities in access to financial services and may exclude certain groups of consumers

The investigation identified internet access, mobile data costs and digital literacy as important barriers. These barriers can affect consumers differently, particularly people in lower-income areas and consumers who are less familiar with digital technology.

    this may include:
  • High mobile data costs
  • Limited internet access
  • Unreliable connectivity
  • Lack of digital literacy

Key Findings

  • Online banking provides greater convenience and accessibility.
  • Cyber-fraud remains an important security concern.
  • Internet access and data costs can create barriers.
  • Digital literacy affects how easily consumers can use online banking safely.
  • Different consumer groups experience online banking differently.
